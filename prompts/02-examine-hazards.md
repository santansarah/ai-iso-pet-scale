# Task 2 — Examine hazards and their relationship to the requirements

> Help me develop a hazard-considerations document using the attached user requirements specification and example hazard document.
>
> Discuss whether ISO 14971’s medical-device risk-management principles are an appropriate reference for this project. Distinguish using those principles as guidance from demonstrating compliance or completing a full risk-management process.
>
> Review the product’s intended use, workflows, scope, and requirements. Explore potential failures, foreseeable misuse, hazardous situations, and possible harms. Ask me one focused question at a time where a decision or clarification is needed.
>
> Give each hazard consideration a stable ID. Record the possible failure, hazardous situation, consequence, and relevant requirement and acceptance criterion IDs. Explain where existing requirements address a concern and where coverage is missing or uncertain.
>
> Don’t invent risk ratings, assume a risk is acceptable, or describe a proposed safeguard as implemented or verified. Flag gaps and propose requirement changes for my review; don’t silently rewrite the requirements.
>
> Finish by checking that the documents use consistent terminology and valid references. Identify unresolved concerns and the future testing or evidence needed to assess the proposed safeguards.
>
> Present the result in an easy-to-scan format consistent with the requirements document.
